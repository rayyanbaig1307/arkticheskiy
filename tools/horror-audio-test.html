АРКТИЧЕСКИЙ · horror sound test

Every sound here is synthesised in code by src/audio/Horror.js (reached in the game as audio.horror); nothing is a recording. Press Start, then click through. Headphones help: you stand at the origin facing forward (−Z) and positional sounds play where the Position list says. The place sets the bed and the reverb (outdoors: the long echo off the ice; inside: a hard room, the bunker's concrete, the cave).
